# behavior-transform.io Product

behavior-transform.io is a pre/post inference compliance intermediary for
authorized operators. It translates sensitive operational language into
provider-compliant form before inference and delivers clean responses afterward.

## Users

- Law enforcement, government, and military teams using AI models for
  operational work that falls within their internal authorization but outside
  a model provider's default content policy.
- Authorized security operations teams (penetration testing, red team,
  adversary emulation) whose assessment language triggers provider refusals
  under standard-tier access.
- Enterprise compliance teams that need a single auditable boundary between
  their organization and multiple AI model providers.

## Product Promise

One compliance boundary between authorized operators and any number of model
providers. The tool handles vocabulary translation, authorization framing,
policy-gate logic, and audit trail in one place, replacing the N-to-N
relationship of per-provider enterprise agreements with a single integration
surface.

## Evidence

The product emits doctor/demo receipts, hook checks, profile state, channel
identity, mode verdicts, hashes, counts, and redacted references.
